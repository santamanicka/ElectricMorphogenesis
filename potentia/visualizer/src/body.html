Paint

Painting edits the current tissue, whatever initial condition it started from: run from a homogeneous or random start, pause, paint, and press Run to continue. It does not restart the simulation.

paint
value
brush radius (pixels)
clamp length (steps)

Vmem of painted pixels follows G_pol straight away on a fresh tissue and, on a tissue that has run, wherever g is monostable (below 0.80 or above 1.44); inside the bistable window a running cell keeps its Vmem branch, so the paint is visible in the G_pol view. Painted pixels get a yellow outline in every view until they are applied (Run or +1 s). Only tissue can be painted: pixels outside the shape (the checkerboard), including the part of the grid that a developing tissue has not reached yet, are ignored. Painting Vmem only has a lasting effect where g is bistable. “Initialise” restarts from the background and discards the paint.

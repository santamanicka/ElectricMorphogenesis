Part 2 · Controlled simulations on model 1888

One more order: the gradient

To compare the two orders fairly, both come from the same model, in paired simulations. The dial alone is code = DC. Adding the first order gives code = DC + G·cos(θ − φ): a tilt of strength G pointing in direction φ. Every gradient run has a twin with the same DC and no gradient, so the difference between them is exactly what the first order adds. Model 1888 was chosen because its trained code is 91% order 0. Its pure-dial mode matches the trained-population mode above (r = +0.61).

Turning the gradient

Three differences, measured

The dial switches the gradient on

A correction. Very strong gradients (G = 0.4) become mostly direction-independent: 58% of their response no longer turns with them. It would be natural to read that as the gradient "becoming a dial". It isn't. That direction-independent part is uncorrelated with the dial's own mode at every strength tested (|r| ≤ 0.14). A strong gradient drives a different symmetric pattern, not the dial's.
Scope. Part 1 is correlational across 96 trained seeds. Part 2 uses controlled paired simulations on a single model (1888) and a single initial condition. 1888's dial sits near a transition, which likely makes it more sensitive than typical: a gradient of just 0.02 already moves its pattern 2.6 mV. The full protocol and every derived quantity are defined in Methods below.

Model and simulation · both parts

Tissue
An 11 × 11 lattice with no wrap-around, using the native Stigmergic 11 × 11 parameters: field screen size 4, field strength 1.0, transduction weight 1000, transduction bias 0.0005 and gap-junction strength 0.05. These are identical in all 96 checkpoints used.
Initial state
Uniform Vmem of −9.2 mV in every cell — the same stored condition in every checkpoint.
Boundary clamp
At each iteration of the hold, the polarising conductance of all 40 outer-ring cells is set to its clamp value × G_ref. The code is that list of clamp values, written G_pol/G_ref. The clamp is then removed and the tissue runs freely.
Run
3000 iterations (dt = 0.01, so 30 time units), simulated forward only, with no training. Replays use the same procedure as §12.7's scoring scripts, which reproduced every checked checkpoint's stored training loss to within 0.0012.
Readout
For every run, the per-cell mean of Vmem over iterations 2000–2999, long after release. The tissue never settles and cycles on a scale of roughly 1000–1500 iterations (§12.2), so a late 1000-iteration average reduces, but does not remove, dependence on where in that cycle a run happens to be.
Face outlines
Dashed boxes mark the 14 target feature cells: two 2 × 2 eyes, a three-cell nose and a three-cell mouth.

Part 1 · trained seeds

Seeds
All 96 Gpol-only, depth-1 checkpoints: 12 seeds for each of four training objectives — correlation and globalsum (§12.4), facialFeatureOnly (§12.10) and facialFeatureBalanced (§12.11) — at each of two hold durations, 100 or 301 iterations. Each code is two-fold mirror symmetric and constant during its hold.
Dial
A seed's mean clamp value over its 40 ring cells.
Dial groups
Seeds ranked by dial: the lowest 24 (ranks 1–24), middle 24 (ranks 37–60) and highest 24 (ranks 73–96). A group's pattern is the per-cell mean of its members' readouts.
Change map
Each readout is first centred on its own spatial mean; the map is the highest-24 mean minus the lowest-24 mean. The group-to-group distances (1.8 and 3.6 mV) are RMS differences between centred group means.
Pattern PC2
Readouts are centred on their own spatial mean and each cell is standardised across the 96 seeds before PCA. PC2's sign is set so its score rises with the dial. The r = +0.79 compares the change map with the PC2 loading.
Lobes
The 15 cells with the most negative and the 15 with the most positive PC2 loadings. A lobe's value is its cells' mean centred Vmem, averaged over the lowest or highest 24 seeds.
Dial–mode correlation
ρ between dial and PC2 score. For the configuration-controlled version, both variables are ranked separately within each of the 8 hold × objective cells (12 seeds each), and the pooled ranks are then correlated.
Code PC1
PCA of the 40 ring values across the 96 seeds. Each cell is centred across seeds but not standardised, and each code's own mean is kept so the dial stays in. Harmonic shares are the squared magnitudes of the discrete Fourier coefficients of the loading, walking clockwise from the top-left corner, as a share of orders 0–20; orders 0–8 are shown.

Part 2 · controlled simulations on model 1888

Reference model
Checkpoint 1888 (Gpol-only D1, hold 100, trained on facialFeatureBalanced). Of the 96, it has the most dial-like trained code — 90.6% of its code power in order 0 — while lying close to the population's trend between dial and face score. Its trained code is replaced by synthetic codes; its parameters, initial state, 100-iteration hold and run length are all kept.
Synthetic code
Each ring cell's clamp value is DC + G·cos(θ − φ), where θ is the cell's angle about the lattice centre, measured clockwise from straight up (toward row 0). Values are clipped to [−1, 1]; no run reached the clip.
Paired population
150 pairs, with DC uniform on [−0.10, 0.65], G log-uniform on [0.01, 0.5] and φ uniform on [0, 2π), drawn with random seed 1. Each pair is a gradient run plus a twin with the same DC and G = 0. The differential Δ is the gradient run's readout minus its twin's, cell by cell.
Rotation grid
DC fixed at 1888's own trained mean, 0.381. G takes the values 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 and 0.4, each at φ = 0°, 45°, …, 315° (40 runs), all compared against one shared G = 0 run.
Dial mode
The mean readout of the 15 highest-DC twins minus that of the 15 lowest-DC twins. Its match to Part 1 (r = +0.61) is its correlation with Part 1's change map after centring. The gating chart's dashed line is its RMS, 4.24 mV.
Steerable pair
At each G of the rotation grid, with Δⱼ the change at direction φⱼ: the direction-independent part is the mean of Δⱼ over the 8 directions, and the steerable pair is U = ¼ Σ Δⱼ cos φⱼ (top–bottom) and V = ¼ Σ Δⱼ sin φⱼ (left–right). The quoted shares are each part's fraction of Σ‖Δⱼ‖². The mode figure and the filmstrip use G = 0.1.
Symmetric share
A map's energy after averaging it over the 8 symmetries of the square (4 rotations and their mirror images), divided by its original energy. The "gradient changes" dot summarises the 64 pairs whose differential exceeded 1 mV RMS; each mode dot uses that mode's loading.
Reach by shell
Shells are concentric square rings, from shell 0 (the 40 boundary cells) to shell 5 (the centre cell). Values are RMS over each shell's cells: of the dial mode for the dial, and the median over the 30 pairs with G ≥ 0.2 for the strong gradient.
Dimensions
Participation ratio (Σλ)² / Σλ² over the full PCA eigenvalue spectrum — of the 150 twin readouts for the dial, and of the 150 differentials for the gradient.
Differential modes
PCA of the 150 differentials, 8 components, with loadings normalised to ±1. Controllability is the 6-fold cross-validated R² of RBF kernel ridge regression, with regularisation and kernel width chosen by an inner 3-fold grid search, predicting a mode's score from DC, log G, G·cos φ, G·sin φ, cos φ, sin φ and DC·G. A mode counts as controlled when its R² exceeds both 0.2 and the best of 10 label-shuffled runs. It "tracks" a setting when |ρ| ≥ 0.2 with cos φ (top–bottom direction), sin φ (left–right direction), log G (strength) or DC.
Gating
A pair's effect is the RMS of Δ over all 121 cells. The dial bands are DC below 0.15, 0.15–0.40, and 0.40 or above; the quoted medians use pairs with G ≥ 0.05.

1. Model Architecture
Gene Regulatory Network Topology
The GRN consists of three morphogen gradients (SHH, FGF8, EDN1) that drive the expression of eight genes organized into feature-specific cascades. Eye genes form a 4-level cascade (rx→six3→pax6→lhx2), while nose (alx) and mouth (dlx→hand2) have simpler architectures. Bone (runx2) is the default state.
